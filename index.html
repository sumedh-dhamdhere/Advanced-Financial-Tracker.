ABOUT CAPITA

Advanced Financial Tracker

v3 · Aurora Edition

Capita is a focused personal-finance workspace designed to make everyday money management simpler, clearer, and more controlled. It brings transactions, financial insights, currency estimates, and private file storage together in one clean dashboard.

TransactionsAdd, edit, search, filter, and review income and expenses.
AnalyticsUnderstand spending patterns and weekly cash-flow performance.
CurrencyQuickly estimate values across USD, EUR, GBP, INR, and JPY.
Secure VaultPreview, rename, download, and delete locally encrypted files.

Privacy-first by design: your financial records and vault files stay in your browser. Capita uses local browser storage for the finance workspace and an encrypted IndexedDB vault for stored files. No account or cloud sync is required for the core experience.
